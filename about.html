Our Process

From concept to your hands

  1. 1

    Submit a request or enquiry

    Get in touch and tell us what you're looking for. Share your design, an idea or just your requirements.

  2. 2

    Receive your mock-up and quote

    We'll send you a custom mock-up and quote.

    Within 24 hours
  3. 3 You decide

    Choose your next step

    You can either:

    • Order a physical sample to check your design (paid sample), or
    • Place your order with us (deposit required).
  4. 4

    Receive your order photo

    After your deposit is paid, we make a sample of your design and send you a photo of it for approval.

  5. 5 You decide

    Approve the design

    Once you're happy with the photo, give us your approval and we'll move to production.

  6. 6

    Bulk production

    We get your order into production and handle the quality checks.

  7. 7

    We ship it out

    Once production is complete, we pack it up and ship it to you, Australia-wide.

What We Stand For

Our values & approach

Based In

Brisbane, Australia

We're a locally run, Australian-staffed business operating out of Brisbane. Everything is managed locally, no offshore customer service, no communication delays, no lost-in-translation briefs. Local decoration, local fulfilment, and a team that understands the market you're operating in.

We partner with reputable Australian and New Zealand suppliers who share our commitment to quality. That means consistent product standards, reliable lead times, and merchandise that genuinely reflects your brand.

Personal, End-to-End Service

A dedicated person manages your order from first enquiry to merchandise at your door. One contact, clear communication, and someone who genuinely cares about the outcome.

Quality First

We only work with reputable suppliers and back every order with a 100% quality guarantee. If it's not right, we fix it, no arguments, no delays.

Transparent Pricing

Upfront quotes with no hidden fees. What you see is what you pay, every time. We believe trust starts with honesty about cost.

See Before You Commit

Every order includes a complimentary photo sample before production begins. You approve it, then we go. No surprises at the finish line.
