Our Process

From concept to your hands

A simple, straightforward process to get your custom merch, on time and on brand. Hover over each step to see what happens next.Tap each step to see what happens next.

  1. 1

    Submit a request or enquiry

    Get in touch and tell us what you're looking for. Share your design, an idea or just your requirements.

  2. 2

    Receive your mock-up and quote

    We'll send you a custom mock-up and quote.

    Within 24 hours
  3. 3

    Choose your next step

    You can either:

    • Order a physical sample to check your design (paid sample), or
    • Place your order with us.
  4. 4

    Pay your deposit

    Once you place your order, we'll send an invoice for your deposit to get things started.

  5. 5

    Receive your order photo

    After your deposit is paid, we make a sample of your design and send you a photo of it for approval.

  6. 6

    Approve the design

    Once you're happy with the photo, give us your approval and we'll move to production.

  7. 7

    Bulk production

    We get your order into production and handle the quality checks.

  8. 8

    We ship it out

    Once production is complete, we pack it up and ship it to you, Australia-wide.
