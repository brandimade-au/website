Referral Rewards, Terms & Conditions

1 Eligibility

  • The Program is open to all existing Brandi Made customers.
  • A "Referrer" is any current or past Brandi Made customer who introduces a new client to Brandi Made.
  • The Referred Client must be a new customer who has not previously placed an order with Brandi Made under any name or company.

2 Referral Reward

  • When a Referred Client places their first order of 50 units or more, both parties are rewarded.
  • The Referrer receives a $120 EFTPOS gift card, or a $120 credit toward their next Brandi Made order if they prefer.
  • The Referred Client receives $100 off their first order. Gift cards are not available to Referred Clients.
  • Credits are applied as a deduction from total order value, exclusive of GST and shipping.
  • There is no expiry date on referral credits.

3 How to Qualify

  • The Referred Client must clearly mention the Referrer's full name and company name at the time of enquiry or before confirming their first order.
  • Referrals claimed after an order is placed cannot be retrospectively applied.
  • Referrals may be submitted via the Referral Form on this page, or mentioned directly in an enquiry email.

4 Multiple Referrals

  • There is no limit to the number of referrals a customer can make.
  • Credits are earned per successful referral and can be combined toward a single future order.

5 Credit Use

  • Referral credits can only be used for Brandi Made products and services.
  • Credits are non-transferable and have no cash value.
  • Referrers choose between the $120 EFTPOS gift card and the $120 order credit once the referral is approved.

6 Program Modifications

  • Brandi Made reserves the right to amend or end the Program at any time without prior notice.
  • Any changes will not affect credits already earned prior to the date of change.

7 Fair Use

  • Brandi Made reserves the right to decline or revoke credits if there is evidence of misuse, self-referral, or fraudulent activity.
  • Decisions made by Brandi Made regarding eligibility or disputes are final.

8 Contact

For any questions regarding referral eligibility or credit application, contact service@brandimade.com.au
