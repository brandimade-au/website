The Process

Simple from start
to delivery.

No hidden fees. No surprises. Just a clear, managed process from your first enquiry through to branded merchandise at your door.

Step by Step

How it works

01

Send Your Enquiry

Tell us what you're after, product type, quantity, your logo or design, and any deadlines. No brief is too rough. We'll work with what you've got and ask the right questions from there.

Quick turnaround

Fill out the quote form and we'll respond within 24 hours. The more detail you give us, the faster we can turn around an accurate quote.

You'll be assigned a dedicated sales manager from the start, one contact, no handoffs.

02

Receive Your Tailored Quote

We provide a clear, itemised quote, upfront pricing with no hidden fees. You'll see exactly what you're paying for: product cost, decoration, freight, and any applicable GST.

No hidden fees

Our quotes are fully transparent. What you see is what you pay, no surprise charges at invoice.

We'll also send through a complimentary digital mock-up so you can see exactly how your branding will look on the product before committing.

03

Approve Your Photo Sample

Before bulk production begins, we provide a complimentary photo sample of your actual branded product. You see exactly what's being produced and approve it before we proceed.

Included with every order

This is included with every order at no extra cost, so you have full confidence in the product before it goes into production.

Need to hold it in your hands? A physical sample can also be arranged for an additional fee.

04

Confirm & We Produce

Once you're happy with the sample, we proceed to full production. Our local team manages the decoration and fulfilment process end-to-end, you don't need to chase anyone.

Local production

Local stock orders typically take 3–4 weeks from approval to delivery. Custom overseas products may take up to 12 weeks, we'll set clear timelines upfront.

Your sales manager keeps you updated throughout so you're never left wondering where things are at.

05

Delivered to Your Door

Your branded merchandise is shipped Australia-wide, directly to you or your team. We handle all logistics, you just unwrap and enjoy.

Australia-wide delivery

We deliver to all major cities and regional areas. Shipping costs are included in your quote, no surprises at checkout.

Not right? Our 100% quality guarantee means we fix it. Simple.
